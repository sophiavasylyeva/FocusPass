# FocusPass — Project Context

## What FocusPass is

FocusPass is a screen-time management app for kids built by Sophia Vasylyeva (founder, college student). Core mechanic: kids **earn screen time by completing productive real-life tasks** (chores, reading, practicing an instrument, yardwork). Phone time is the reward, tied to existing daily habits — not new learning content.

**Positioning wedge:** Blockers (Apple Screen Time, Bark, Opal) restrict without rewarding and are easily bypassed. Reward apps (Joon) never enforce actual screen-time limits. FocusPass is the only solution that does both: enforces limits *and* makes screen time the reward. Themes: empowering the parent-child relationship, making phone time intentional.

**Target users:** Parents of **11–15 year olds** — customer discovery showed this window has dramatically higher parent-child conflict; by 16, parents broadly consider full phone autonomy appropriate.

- Working with marketing coach James ([james@gorogue.net](mailto:james@gorogue.net), Go Rogue); assigned milestone: 40 in-person parent interviews  
- 21 customer discovery touchpoints so far (11 in-person parent conversations \+ 10 survey responses), after pivoting from cold approaches (NorthPark mall) to "watering holes": HP High School, Snider Plaza tutoring services, former high school PTA, warm intros.

  - Existing parental controls: too hard to set up, too easy to bypass, not customizable  
  - Verbal agreements are the default fallback but break down as kids age  
  - Parents independently describe a points-for-minutes model tied to chores — validating the core mechanic unprompted  
  - Unprompted willingness to pay \~$10/month  
  - New pain point: school-issued laptops during summer → possible web version of FocusPass

## Competitive landscape

- **Direct:** Apple Screen Time / Google Family Link, Joon, Bark, Opal  
- **Indirect (blocking):** Forest, One Sec, Brick  
- **Indirect (reward):** Habitica, Finch, chore-reward platforms, Duolingo, Epic  
- Known shared risk: novelty decay — gamified streak/reward systems (e.g. Joon) lose engagement after weeks; FocusPass must plan for this proactively
